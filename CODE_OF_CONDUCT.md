# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free research and development
community. Be constructive, assume good faith, respect privacy, and critique
ideas rather than people. Do not publish credentials, private communications,
participant information or security details in issues or pull requests.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to `mili4@meei.harvard.edu`. Project
maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
necessary to protect the community or research participants.
